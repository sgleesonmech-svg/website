Who we are

One of the region’s largest stockists of valves, pumps & instrumentation.

Leesonmech Singapore Pte Ltd is one of the largest stockists for distribution of valves, pumps, instrumentation, control and safety products, with a regional presence in Singapore, Malaysia (East & West), The Philippines, Indonesia, Thailand, Papua New Guinea and Ivory Coast.

We are also the representative of Okumura (Japan), Speck Pumpen / Circutec (Germany), Meidinger (Switzerland) and Yasiki (Malaysia), supplying industries and projects across the region.
