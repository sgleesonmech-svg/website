About Leesonmech

A long-term partner in flow control.

Stockist and distributor of valves, pumps, instrumentation, control and safety products for Singapore and the region.

Company profile

One of the largest stockists in the region.

Leesonmech Singapore Pte Ltd is one of the largest stockists for distribution of valves, pumps, instrumentation, control and safety products, with our regional presence in Singapore, Malaysia (East & West), The Philippines, Indonesia, Thailand, Papua New Guinea and Ivory Coast.

We are also the representative of Okumura (Japan), Speck Pumpen / Circutec (Germany), Meidinger (Switzerland) and Yasiki (Malaysia). Leesonmech Singapore Pte Ltd supplies to various industries and projects across the region.
